Privacy policy

Last updated 28 September 2026.

Personal Assistant is used by one person, its developer, Amirhossein Daraee. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With his permission given through Google sign-in, it reads his own Google data: sleep, heart rate and activity from Fitbit (Google Health), and his calendar and email. Access is read-only.

What it does with it

The data is used only to write his daily summary. It is stored on his own computer, is not sold, is not shared with anyone, and is not used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data is deleted by removing it from that computer.

Google user data is handled in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
