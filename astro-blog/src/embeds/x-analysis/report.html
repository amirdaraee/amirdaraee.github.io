The Mia Plays and Koroush shows in Canada in October 2026 were followed by a fight on X: reviews of the singing, insults, defenses of the singers, and political arguments.

We searched public posts from about 4 to 11 October for both names, phrases from the shows, lyric quotes, and a short slur query. That left 917 original posts. Retweets are out, and there is no follow graph.

Each post got one of ten labels. Insult or shame means it degrades the person, even if it also judges the singing. A rude line about the work alone is performance critique. Then we counted posts, likes and views by label and by account.
